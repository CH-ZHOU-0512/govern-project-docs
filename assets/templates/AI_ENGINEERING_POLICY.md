# AI engineering and change records

AI follows repository instructions and reads context progressively. Efficiency does not authorize skipping directly relevant safety, privacy, tenant, contract, migration, or acceptance constraints.

Create one independent change archive when a change crosses domains, modifies contracts or data, affects security, permissions, concurrency, or audit, changes infrastructure or providers, fixes a non-obvious recurring defect, or performs a major refactor or compatibility tradeoff.

Do not create archives for simple copy edits, formatting, generated refreshes, or low-risk local fixes. Archives record facts, verification, limitations, unverified work, and rollback. They do not accept decisions, change product scope, or turn automated checks into device or external acceptance.
